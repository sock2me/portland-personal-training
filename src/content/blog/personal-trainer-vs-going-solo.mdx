Every January and September, thousands of Portlanders sign up for gym memberships with the best intentions. Yet statistics show that over 67% of gym memberships go completely unused within the first 90 days.

Why is consistency so challenging on your own, and what happens when you introduce dedicated 1-on-1 coaching?

### The Solo Gym Paradox

When you go to a gym alone without a structured program, several obstacles quickly arise:

1. **Decision Fatigue:** Wandering around looking for open machines drains mental energy before you even begin working out.
2. **Technique Uncertainty:** Wondering if your squat or hinge form is damaging your lower back creates hesitation.
3. **No External Accountability:** When a workout is only on your mental checklist, it is easy to skip when work runs late.

### How Coaching Changes the Math

Research in behavioral psychology and exercise science consistently demonstrates that individuals working with a personal trainer achieve significantly higher adherence rates.

### Precision Progression
A qualified coach ensures that every set and rep is calibrated to your current recovery capacity. You never waste energy on junk volume or push past safe thresholds.

### Immediate Form Feedback
Small adjustments in ribcage positioning, foot angle, or grip can turn an exercise that hurts your shoulder into one that safely builds strength.
