## Visual Theme
Fresh, welcoming, and high-energy Portland athletic aesthetic on a crisp white backdrop. Alternating bands of soft blue and light grey separate content sections, energized by vibrant azure blue (#009fe6) and sunset orange CTA buttons and accents.

## Art Direction
- Concept: Welcoming, zero-pressure athletic community with clean daylight readability, structured cards, and high-contrast blue and orange button hierarchy.
- Layout rhythm: Dynamic alternating bands (crisp white, soft grey-blue surface, and subtle sky tint) with generous breathing room and elevated cards.

## Typography
- Scale: display
- Headlines: Plus Jakarta Sans — energetic, bold geometric sans with confident athletic authority
- Body: Inter — crisp, universally legible neutral sans for dense form and reading clarity

## Components
- Motif: Clean rounded cards, subtle light grey-blue container borders, and high-contrast badge pills.
- Buttons: Rounded-full pills with bold font weights adopting primary azure blue (#009fe6) and vibrant sunset orange (#f97316) for dual CTA impact.
- Containers: Crisp white elevated cards with soft border definitions and subtle shadows.
- Accent architecture: Azure blue (#009fe6) drives core navigation and primary trial conversions; sunset orange highlights high-intent actions, hiring, and secondary CTA triggers.
- Sections: Alternating between crisp white daylight (`bg-background`), soft blue-grey tints (`bg-surface`), and pale sky washes (`bg-sky-50/50`).
- Imagery: Crisp documentary-style personal training photography with rounded-2xl geometry.

## Do's and Don'ts
- DO celebrate real body diversity, age ranges (women 50+), and LGBTQ+ welcoming community.
- DO keep the free session form front-and-center with zero friction.
- DON'T use generic ultra-shredded bodybuilding clichés or sterile clinical hospital aesthetics.
- DON'T obscure the 8-year Lloyd Athletic Club award or zero-commission policy.
